# Dr M Khan Home

Interactive 3D model of the Dr. Muhammad Khan clinic and residence (Soomro Revised-01, Masson Road, Dadu), built from the Dar Associates ground-floor and first-floor plans. Plot 30′ × 60′.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (needs internet for the Three.js CDN). No build step.

## Controls
- Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom, two fingers or right-drag to pan
- Views: 3D, Plan (top), Front, Side
- Floor filter, roof, labels, furniture, X-ray, night mode
- Explode slider separates the floors; Cut slider slices the model at a chosen height
- Tap a room to see its name, size and area
- Walk inside: joystick or WASD, drag to look, doors open as you approach, stairs are walkable

## Notes
Room positions and sizes follow the plan drawings. Window positions, wall heights (10′-6″ per floor), furniture and the roof tank are assumed, since the plans do not show them.
